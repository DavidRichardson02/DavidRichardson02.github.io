01 Active development

Basys 3 · Verilog · VGA · I²C/SPI/UART

CaelumFusion Flight Control System

A Basys 3 avionics instrumentation stack built around deterministic sensor snapshots, explicit provenance and freshness, CDC-safe publication, VGA diagnostic surfaces, and host-visible evidence.

  • Board-facing integration through caelumfusion_top_vga.
  • Raw and derived telemetry banks carry validity, status, sequence, age, and source information.
  • Includes fixed-point estimator and apogee-authority policy RTL, focused benches, black-box frame packing, and scripted Vivado flows.
Present evidence boundary Integrated source, project metadata, targeted testbenches, and release checklists are present. Fresh matching-version synthesis, implementation, timing closure, and complete hardware signoff remain explicit gates.

02 Canonical analysis package

MATLAB · Telemetry · Estimator replay

Offline Flight Analysis Dashboard

The offline counterpart to the flight-control stack: import, schema normalization, estimator replay, engineering dashboards, and firmware-to-analysis contract validation for captured SD and serial telemetry.

  • Vertical-state replay with innovation, covariance, bias, beta, and gating diagnostics.
  • Integrated review surfaces for sensor health, uncertainty, GPS, trajectory, wind, mission phase, policy, and provenance.
  • A release entry point runs firmware alignment, replay, mission-profile, and live-import validation gates.

03 Phase 4 integration

Nexys Video · HDMI · Camera · Sonar

AquaFusion Sonar Vision System

A multi-clock sonar and camera fusion HUD for the Nexys Video, combining Pcam 5C control, sonar publication, occupancy-map painting, frame-committed telemetry, HDMI composition, OLED output, and dedicated debug surfaces.

  • Region-qualified HDMI composition replaces unsafe black-pixel transparency.
  • SYS-to-VID snapshot CDC and frame-boundary commits keep telemetry coherent on screen.
  • Isolated camera, sonar-map, UART, radar, painter, and status views support staged bring-up.
Known limits The camera viewport is a raster clip rather than a scaler; Sonar 2 is tied off; bearing validity is not yet supplied by the live fusion path; CLS integration is currently commented out.

About

Engineering from interfaces inward.

I am a Computer Engineering undergraduate at the University of Michigan–Dearborn, focused on embedded systems, FPGA design, numerical estimation, telemetry, and scientific visualization.

My projects typically begin at a real interface—a sensor bus, an asynchronous control, a telemetry packet, a pixel stream, or a scientific dataset—and proceed through explicit contracts until the resulting behavior can be measured. That approach has led me across RTL, firmware, MATLAB analysis, C/C++ simulation, and the instrumentation required to connect them.

I am interested in engineering and research environments where timing, observability, numerical behavior, and careful verification matter as much as feature completion.
